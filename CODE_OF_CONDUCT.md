# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and harassment-free community for every participant,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Assume good intent while taking security and safety concerns seriously.
- Respect privacy, coordinated disclosure, and requests to stop contact.
- Accept responsibility, apologize for harm, and improve behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, deliberate
misrepresentation, unsafe disclosure of vulnerabilities, or publishing another person's private
information is not acceptable. Using project spaces to facilitate credential theft, market
manipulation, malware, or harm to others is prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, reviews, issues, community spaces, events, and when a
person officially represents Verus.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/EcstaceeLOR/Verus/security/advisories/new) and label the
report as a conduct concern. Do not expose a target's identity in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports as confidentially and promptly as practical. They may remove
content, issue a warning, restrict participation, temporarily suspend access, or permanently ban a
participant according to context, impact, and pattern. Maintainers with a conflict of interest must
recuse themselves. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.

This project policy is informed by widely used open-source community standards and may be refined as
the maintainer team grows.
